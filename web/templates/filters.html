    Required matches

    Blocked content

    Any match rejects the title. Keywords match partial title text; IDs match one exact title.

    Content ratings

    Evaluate the rating issued in one country. TMDB supplies regional movie certifications and TV content ratings.

    Rules stay inactive until an allowed or blocked rating is added. Unknown means TMDB has no rating for the selected country.

    Allow overrides

    Any match accepts the title after block rules, bypassing required matches and boundaries. Preview before enabling automation.
